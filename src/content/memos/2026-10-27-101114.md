    content: "For middle-aged and older adults, particularly those over 65, sports injuries harbor a profound hidden risk: if a severe injury forces them into prolonged bed rest, muscle mass and strength deteriorate rapidly—decaying by up to 15% in a single week—initiating a downward death spiral where the weaker one grows, the less one moves, and the less one moves, the weaker one becomes. A critical mechanism underpins this: older adults, especially those who abstain from strength training, develop anabolic resistance, becoming desensitized to muscle protein synthesis signals so that dietary protein yields minimal muscle growth. Bed rest further accelerates this decline. From this vantage point, moderate strength training holds a significance for middle-aged and older adults that far surpasses aerobic exercise like running. Pursuing exercise while ensuring total immunity from injury is, structurally, precisely akin to engaging in investment while strictly forbidding the use of leverage."

运动受伤对于中老年人，尤其是65岁以上的人，还有一个潜在风险：如果严重受伤导致卧床不起，肌肉和力量会衰减得非常快，卧床一周的衰减可以高达 15%, 然后就进入一个死亡螺旋：越弱越不愿动，越不动就越弱。这里面有个关键机制：老年人，尤其是不做力量训练的老年人，会有合成阻抗 (anabolic resistance), 对肌肉蛋白质合成讯号变得迟钝，吃了蛋白质也不怎么长肌肉。而卧床不起会加剧肌肉蛋白质合成的退化。从这个角度看，适度力量训练的重要性，对于中老年人说，远远超过跑步之类的有氧运动。 又要运动，又要运动不受伤，结构上就好像又要做投资，又绝对不能用杠杆。
